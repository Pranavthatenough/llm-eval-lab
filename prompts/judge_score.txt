## Scoring scale
5 = Fully correct and complete. Nothing important missing, nothing wrong.
4 = Correct, with a minor omission or slightly awkward wording.
3 = Partly correct: some real errors OR important parts missing.
2 = Mostly wrong or unhelpful, with a small correct element.
1 = Wrong, made up, harmful, or does not answer the question.

## Rules
- Judge correctness and substance, NOT length or confidence. A short correct answer beats a long wrong one.
- Any invented fact (a name, number, date or source that is not true) caps the score at 2.
- If the answer says it does not know, that is better than a confident wrong answer (score 2-3 unless the question was clearly answerable).
- Think step by step in "reasoning" BEFORE deciding the score.
