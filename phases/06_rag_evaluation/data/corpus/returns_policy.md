# Returns Policy

Unused items in their original packaging can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Summit Club Gold members get an extended 60-day return window.

Items marked "Final Sale" on the product page cannot be returned or exchanged, even if unused. Gift cards are also non-returnable.

Refunds go back to the original payment method and appear within 5 to 7 business days after the warehouse receives the return.

Return shipping is free if the item is defective or we sent the wrong item. For all other returns a $6.95 return shipping fee is deducted from the refund.
