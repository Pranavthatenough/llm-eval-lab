# Shipping

Standard shipping is free on orders over $75. Orders of $75 or less pay a flat $5.99 for standard shipping. Standard shipping takes 3 to 6 business days.

Expedited shipping costs $14.99 and arrives in 2 business days. Summit Club Gold members get expedited shipping for free.

We currently ship only to addresses in the United States and Canada. Canadian orders take 5 to 10 business days, and import duties are paid by the customer on delivery.

Items heavier than 30 lb (such as large tents and kayaks) cannot be shipped to PO boxes.
