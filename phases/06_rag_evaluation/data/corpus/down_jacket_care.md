# Caring for Down Jackets

Wash down jackets in a front-loading washing machine on a gentle cycle with cold water and a down-specific detergent. Top-loading machines with an agitator can damage the baffles.

Tumble dry on low heat with two or three clean tennis balls to break up clumps of down. Drying fully can take two to three hours.

Never dry clean a down jacket; the solvents strip the natural oils from the down. Re-apply a DWR (durable water repellent) treatment about every six months of regular use.
