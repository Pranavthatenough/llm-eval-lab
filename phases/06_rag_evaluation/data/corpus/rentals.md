# Gear Rentals

Gear rentals are available only at the one store that runs our rental program (see Our Stores).

Prices: tents cost $25 per day or $60 for a weekend (Friday to Monday). Sleeping bags cost $15 per day. Backpacks cost $12 per day.

Every rental requires a $100 refundable deposit. Renters must be at least 18 years old and show a photo ID. Gear returned damaged beyond normal use is charged a repair fee.
