# Warranty

The Pinecrest Lifetime Promise covers manufacturing defects on Pinecrest-brand gear for the lifetime of the product. If a seam, zipper, buckle or pole fails because of a defect, we will repair or replace it at no cost.

The Lifetime Promise does not cover normal wear and tear, accidents, misuse, or damage from improper care.

Electronics, including Pinecrest headlamps and GPS units, have a 2-year limited warranty instead of the Lifetime Promise.

Products from other brands sold by Pinecrest follow that manufacturer's own warranty. All warranty claims require proof of purchase.
