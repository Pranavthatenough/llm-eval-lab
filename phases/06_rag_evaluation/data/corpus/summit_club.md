# Summit Club Loyalty Program

Summit Club is free to join. Members earn 1 point for every $1 spent. Every 100 points can be redeemed for a $5 reward. Rewards expire 12 months after they are issued.

Members who spend more than $1,000 in a calendar year reach Gold status for the following year.

Gold members earn 2 points per $1 and receive extra shipping and returns benefits, described in our Shipping and Returns policies.
